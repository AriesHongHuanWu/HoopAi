# Hoopilot — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 8 July 2026**

**Hoopilot processes everything on your phone. Your camera feed and session
videos are analysed on-device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else. We
run no ads, no third-party trackers and no analytics. Nothing leaves your phone
unless you tap Share or Export yourself.**

## Who we are

Hoopilot is an on-device basketball shot tracker. This policy explains what data
the app touches, where it stays, and the few cases where you — and only you —
can send something off the device.

## The camera and microphone

Hoopilot uses your camera to detect the ball and rim and score your shots in
real time, and the microphone to record court audio alongside your session
videos. This all happens live, on the device. Frames are analysed and discarded;
we do not send any camera or microphone data anywhere.

## What is stored on your phone

Hoopilot keeps the following locally, in the app's own storage:

- Session stats and shot history (makes, misses, angles, streaks).
- Recorded clips of your shots, if you keep clips on. You choose whether clips
  are saved and can turn them off entirely in Settings.
- Your player profile — an optional nickname, and optional height, weight,
  wingspan and birth year. Every field is skippable.
- App settings and preferences.

This data lives on your device. It is not synced to a server, and we have no
account system today, so there is no copy of it anywhere else.

## Your profile and fitness data

If you choose to enter height, weight, wingspan or birth year, Hoopilot treats
these as health and fitness details. They are stored only on your phone, used
only to personalise coaching copy and make fair peer comparisons, and are **not
linked to your identity, not used for advertising, and never used to track
you**. You can clear them any time from your profile, and the app never requires
them.

## When something leaves your phone (only if you choose)

The only way anything leaves your device is when you deliberately act:

- **Share** a highlight clip or share card — this hands the file to the share
  sheet (Messages, Instagram, etc.) that you pick.
- **Export** a CSV of your stats, or save a clip to your photo library — the
  file goes where you send it.
- **Contact support** by email — anything you write in that email is sent by
  you, through your own mail app.

Once you share or export a file, how it is handled is governed by the service or
person you sent it to, not by us.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not upload your videos, images or audio.
- We do not run ads or embed advertising SDKs.
- We do not use third-party analytics or tracking.
- We do not sell or share your data — there is nothing to sell.
- We do not create advertising or tracking profiles about you.

## Photos and media library

If you save a highlight to your photo library, or pick an existing basketball
video to analyse, Hoopilot uses the media-library and photo-picker permissions
for exactly that action. It reads only the video you select and writes only the
clips you ask it to save.

## Children

Hoopilot is rated for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal
information from children. Because everything stays on the device and there are
no accounts, the app does not build a profile of any user, child or adult.

## Future changes: accounts, sync and optional telemetry

Hoopilot has no accounts, cloud sync, or crash/usage reporting today. If we add
any of these later, they will be introduced honestly:

- Any crash or performance reporting will be **opt-in**, clearly described, and
  never tied to your video.
- If accounts and cloud sync ship, you will be able to **delete your account and
  its data from inside the app**.
- This policy will be updated before any such feature turns on.
